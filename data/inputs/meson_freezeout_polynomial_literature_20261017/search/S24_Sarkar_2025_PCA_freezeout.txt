                                                              We introduce a Principal Component Analysis (PCA)–Bayesian framework for extracting chemical freeze-out
                                                           conditions in relativistic heavy-ion collisions that resolves long-standing ambiguities in hadron-ratio–based
                                                           analyses. By constructing all possible hadron-yield ratios from a chosen set of species and transforming them
                                                           into an orthogonal PCA basis, the method removes linear redundancies and eliminates the information loss
                                                           and systematic uncertainties associated with ratio selection. Energy-wise Bayesian calibration of the Hadron
                                                           Resonance Gas (HRG) model is then performed directly in this decorrelated space, with a Gaussian Process
                                                           emulator enabling fast and accurate model evaluations. A detailed Sobol sensitivity analysis, together with
                                                           the PCA loading structure, identifies the most informative ratio combinations and reveals a transition from

                                                           model reproduces all measured ratios, and the extracted freeze-out parameters are consistent with previous HRG
                                                           determinations.
