Charged kaon-to-pion ratio along two chemical-freezeout parameterizations. Solid blue: Cleymans et al. (2006); dashed orange: Andronic et al. (2018), both with their published central parameters. Curves show directly computed PNJL+GBU local density ratios with the q0_lambda_reference prescription, quark-only BQS constraints nS=0 and nQ/nB=0.4, xi=0, and a finite Bose window starting at 1e-5 fm^-1. Straight segments connect adjacent valid calculation points; failures remain gaps. These are diagnostic finite-window results, without a final-state decay or rapidity-acceptance model. Filled red circles: published central Au+Au AGS/STAR midrapidity data; filled black squares: NA49 Pb+Pb midrapidity data. Open triangles: ALICE Pb+Pb charge-summed (K+ + K-)/(pi+ + pi-) ratios, used only as auxiliary high-energy observations. The red cross is a HADES point digitized from the supplied compilation; its pixel uncertainty is not an experimental error bar. Other bars combine available statistical and systematic uncertainties in quadrature; the STAR 14.5 GeV ratio is derived from published yields with zero-covariance error propagation. Source-specific centralities, rapidity windows and uncertainties are retained in the experimental input table.
