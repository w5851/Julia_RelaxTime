# Author-accepted freezeout figures: vector delivery

Accepted on 2026-11-08. Each PDF preserves the accepted PNG appearance and frozen numerical inputs.

## Published parameter sets

Charged kaon-to-pion ratio along two chemical-freezeout parameterizations. Solid blue: Cleymans et al. (2006); dashed orange: Andronic et al. (2018), both with their published central parameters. Curves show directly computed PNJL+GBU local density ratios with the q0_lambda_reference prescription, quark-only BQS constraints nS=0 and nQ/nB=0.4, xi=0, and a finite Bose window starting at 1e-5 fm^-1. Straight segments connect adjacent valid calculation points; failures remain gaps. These are diagnostic finite-window results, without a final-state decay or rapidity-acceptance model. Filled red circles and black squares denote AGS/STAR Au+Au and NA49 Pb+Pb midrapidity ratios. Open red circles and black squares denote the full-phase-space AGS/BRAHMS and NA49 energy scan. The purple filled cross is BRAHMS at 62.4 GeV and y=0 (0-10% centrality). Purple open triangles are three STAR BES-II preliminary ratios derived from Fig. 1 of EPJ Web Conf. 296, 14009 (2024): midrapidity pi+ and K+ yields, with the K+ plotting factor 2.5 removed. Their bars propagate the plotted combined yield errors assuming zero kaon-pion covariance; they are derived uncertainties, distinct from the graphical reading interval. The open green diamond is NA61/SHINE preliminary Pb+Pb at 7.62 GeV, 0-7.2% centrality and 4pi acceptance, read from the source vector figure with statistical and systematic errors combined in quadrature. The added reference observations do not enter the frozen parameter scores. Open downward triangles: ALICE Pb+Pb charge-summed (K+ + K-)/(pi+ + pi-) ratios, used only as auxiliary high-energy observations. The red cross is a HADES point digitized from the supplied compilation; its pixel uncertainty is not an experimental error bar. Other bars combine available statistical and systematic uncertainties in quadrature; the STAR 14.5 GeV ratio is derived from published yields with zero-covariance error propagation. Source-specific centralities, rapidity windows and uncertainties are retained in the experimental input table. Only the 14 common valid published Au+Au observations enter the primary equal-weight RMSE comparison. The author-accepted figure is delivered as a vector PDF.
