# Other-Mott local slope diagnostics

Each figure uses the temperature, baryon chemical potential, meson and located threshold of one selected window. Upper panels show directly computed transport values; lower panels show ln(y_R/y_L)/(xi_R-xi_L) at interval midpoints. Coordinates and slopes were tabulated on quote-remote. Blue circles use the refined xi grid with s-table rtol=1e-5; orange squares, if present, identify recomputed original xi nodes only in the upper panel. Black crosses use rtol=1e-6; in the lower panel they compare identical interval endpoints. Green plus signs distinguish split intervals adjoining additional xi nodes at the tighter tolerance. Slopes over unmatched, wider gaps between sparse references are omitted. Lines between observations and secants are visual guides, not fitted derivatives. The vertical gray line is the diagnostic closed-threshold inverse zero; production B0 and mixed-mode governance are unchanged. s-table convergence alone is not an error bound for all integration layers. No smoothing, formal data replacement or publication promotion is performed. Only completed points of the selected diagnostic stages are shown; a pilot figure does not establish completion of the full meson survey. All figures require the full 6.75-inch insertion width.
