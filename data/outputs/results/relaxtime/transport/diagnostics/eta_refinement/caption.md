# Eta reverse-Mott xi refinement

The horizontal coordinate is xi-xi_eta, tabulated remotely relative to each curve's eta threshold. Upper panels show the directly computed transport values. Lower panels show ln(y_right/y_left)/(xi_right-xi_left) at each interval midpoint; connecting segments are guides between these tabulated secants, not a fitted derivative. Orange squares are the original xi nodes recomputed with the same s-sampling tolerance as the blue refined grid. The s-table relative tolerance is 1e-5 for both grids and 1e-6 for the black reference markers; the outer momentum and angular quadrature, inner t quadrature, and production B0 kernel are unchanged. Upper-panel black crosses, where present, include the located threshold and a peak-interval midpoint. In the lower panel, black crosses compare exactly the same interval endpoints as a native fine-grid secant; black plus signs show the newly halved intervals at the tighter tolerance. Slopes over larger gaps between the sparse reference points are not displayed. All shown slopes and their midpoint coordinates were tabulated remotely. The gray vertical line locates the zero of the endpoint-subtracted eta light-mode inverse. This diagnostic locator does not replace the production scattering kernel. No smoothing or replacement of the formal data is performed. The two mu_B=0 curves have slightly different temperatures and are not independent physical repetitions. All panels require the full 6.75-inch insertion width. PNG review only; manuscript eligibility and vector delivery remain pending.
