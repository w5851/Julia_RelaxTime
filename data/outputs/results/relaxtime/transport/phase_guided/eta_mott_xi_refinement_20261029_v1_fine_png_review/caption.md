# Eta reverse-Mott xi refinement

The horizontal coordinate is xi-xi_eta, tabulated remotely relative to each curve's eta threshold. Upper panels show the directly computed transport values. Lower panels show ln(y_right/y_left)/(xi_right-xi_left) at each interval midpoint; connecting segments are guides between these tabulated secants, not a fitted derivative. Orange squares are the original xi nodes recomputed with the same integral tolerance as the blue refined grid. Black crosses, where present, use a tighter integral tolerance and include the exact threshold and an extra peak-interval midpoint. The gray vertical line locates the zero of the endpoint-subtracted eta light-mode inverse. This diagnostic locator does not replace the production scattering kernel. No smoothing or replacement of the formal data is performed. The two mu_B=0 curves have slightly different temperatures and are not independent physical repetitions. All panels require the full 6.75-inch insertion width. PNG review only; manuscript eligibility and vector delivery remain pending.
